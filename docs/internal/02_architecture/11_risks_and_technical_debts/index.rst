----------------------------

   .. container:: formalpara

      “Risk management is project management for grown-ups” (Tim Lister,
      Atlantic Systems Guild.)

   This should be your motto for systematic detection and evaluation of
   risks and technical debts in the architecture, which will be needed
   by management stakeholders (e.g. project managers, product owners) as
   part of the overall risk analysis and measurement planning.
